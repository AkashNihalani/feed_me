# The feed page and the Leader tier (Oct 30 2026)

The feeder page lets someone know an account without opening it. The feed page does the same for a whole space:
competitors, global players and creators read together.

## Two kinds of feed

- **Watchlist**: a feed without you in it. Agencies scouting creators, talent managers watching a roster, brands checking
  who carries an ad, industry research.
- **Arena**: a feed with you in it, you beside your competition. The Leader tier is the Arena with coaching.

## One rule

A fact earns a place on the feed page only if it takes two or more feeders to be true. One account's streak never shows
there; "four of six comedy creators went cold the same week" does.

## Context: moves, not posts

The feed read never reads raw posts from every feeder. Posting rates differ too much (a 100-post window is two months for
one account and five days for another), and a field is about the same moment across accounts.

- **Time windows**: this week, the last 4 weeks, the last 13. Never a post count.
- **Accounts, not posts**: each account counts once per move, and every number is against its own usual post.
- **What the weekly feed call gets** (all built by code):
  - one card per feeder from its own latest read: standing, what moved, standout posts in a line each;
  - the moves table: every format, template, audio, reference, product type and face seen across the feed in the window,
    who used it and when, and how it did for each against their own usual;
  - shared faces and field-wide events (everyone's followers dipping the same week);
  - full fingerprints only for the ~10 posts the story will cite.
- **Size**: ~50K tokens for 20 feeders, ~100K for 50.

### The vocabulary (lock before beta)

Moves must carry one name across accounts. Audio comes from Instagram's own fields. Templates, formats, references (meme,
film, news), faces, product categories and selling signals come from the fingerprint, and a cheap weekly pass merges
synonyms ("POV: the friend who…" and "pov ur friend…" become one move).

### Trends live and die

For each move code knows the order of adopters and how each did:

- **Rising**: one or two accounts, lifting.
- **Peaking**: spreading, still lifting.
- **Worn out**: many adopters, the latest under their usual.

### What selling costs

Each post is scored on how much it looks like an ad: paid label, product time on screen, a pitch to camera, a call to
action. The feed gets what selling costs in its space and who sells without looking like it. Already measured: creators'
paid posts did 0.41× their usual across our feeders; Anuj's pitches sink while his ads inside a bit hold; Lakmē's
model-to-camera demos do 0.5× while Aneet doing a bit does 50–100×.

## The page

- **Story (weekly)**:
  - the move of the week;
  - who had a moment and why;
  - the same move with opposite results;
  - the trend that wore out;
  - a shared face;
  - a field-wide dip;
  - (Leader) where you stand.
- **Strategy**:
  - **Moves**: the trend radar, rising, peaking and worn out, with who started each and who came late.
  - **Momentum**: who's hot and cold against their own usual, with the reason in a line.
  - **Matchups**: the same move for two accounts with opposite results, side by side, and what was different.
  - **Faces**: creators and stars across brands, and who delivers for whom.
  - **Selling**: what looking like an ad costs here.
  - **Moments**: events and seasons, and who they paid off for.
- **Wall**: everyone's posts this week on one wall, each against its own usual.

## Leader tier (₹1,499 for the slot)

The user marks one feeder in a feed as "me" or "my client". On top of the feed page it gets:

1. **The race (weekly)**: rank in the feed on momentum, not size. That means the typical post against its own usual,
   follower growth as a percentage, and its share of the feed's breakouts, plus how the rank moves week to week. Size
   can't be caught; momentum can be won in a month.
2. **The gap**: moves the winners use that you don't, moves you still use that the field has worn out, your cost of
   selling against the field's.
3. **Three moves a week**: each a field move with proof posts, put in the account's own world through its regulars,
   checked next time: tried or not, worked or not, kept as a scorecard.
4. **Early alerts (daily)**: a move rising in the field ("two accounts, both lifting, three days old"), and your own post
   at day 1 against your usual and against the field's same-day posts.
5. **Field twins in your post mortems**: each of your posts beside the closest post anyone in the field made that month.
6. **Ask Feeder Reader**: a chat kept to your feed.

Advice is allowed only here, and only when field posts back it. Extra cost ~₹150–250 a month, inside the ₹500
premium.

## Cadence and price

- **Feed read**: weekly, the same day for every feed, taking each feeder's latest card. About ₹16–20 a read. Free in any
  feed with 3 or more feeders.
- **Leader alerts**: daily. Code first, Sonnet only when a move qualifies.
- **Volume price**: agencies tracking 10 or more creators need one. Shared feeders make it safe, because a feeder already
  tracked by another user costs close to nothing.
