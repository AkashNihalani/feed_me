The Verdict
Violet
Tech-forward, rare in analytics. Naturally premium on both modes. Risk: trendy if overused.
Rose
Warm urgency, fits "fire" brand. Competitive energy. Bold on both backgrounds without tricks.
Orange
Energetic, appetizing (Feed Me!). Warm and bold. Common in analytics — but could feel Strava-like.
Blue
Most trustworthy, professional. Rock-solid contrast. But also the most common — every analytics tool is blue.
Green (current)
Incredible on dark. Broken on light. Forces two design languages. The light mode problem is structural.
